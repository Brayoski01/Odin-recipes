Shoyu Chicken

Shoyu Chicken is a popular hawaiian dish often served with rice. The word shoyu is japanese for soy sauce. Let the chicken soak in the marinade for at least an hour; the longer the better.

Ingredients

  1. 1 Cup soy sauce
  2. 1 cup brown sugar
  3. 1 cup water
  4. 1 onion, chopped
  5. 4 cloves garlic, minced
  6. 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger root
  7. 1 tablespoon ground black pepper
  8. 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  9. 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes(optional)

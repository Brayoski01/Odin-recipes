Smooth sweet Tea

Sweet tea is a classic Southern drink, and this recipe is a breeze to make with your favourite tea. Sweeten the tea while its hot, then keep chilled in the fridge for the perfect cold refreshment to serve ove rice on hot summer days!

Ingredients

  1. 1 pinch baking soda
  2. 2 Cups boiling water
  3. 6 tea bags
  4. 3/4 cup white sugar
  5. 6 cups cool water
